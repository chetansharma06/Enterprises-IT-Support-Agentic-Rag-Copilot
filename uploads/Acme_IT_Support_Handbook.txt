=====================================================
1. VPN ACCESS
=====================================================
Acme Corp uses the "Acme SecureConnect" VPN client for remote access.

How to connect to the company VPN:
1. Install Acme SecureConnect from the Company Portal (Windows and macOS).
2. Open the app and enter the server address: vpn.acmecorp.example
3. Sign in with your company email address and password.
4. Approve the sign-in request in the Acme Authenticator app (MFA is mandatory).
5. Wait for the status to show "Connected" (green shield icon).

VPN troubleshooting:
- If you see error VPN-809, disable any personal VPN or proxy and try again.
- If the app says "MFA timeout", open Acme Authenticator and approve within 60 seconds.
- VPN sessions disconnect automatically after 12 hours of use. Reconnect to continue.
- Split tunneling is disabled. All traffic goes through the company network.

=====================================================
2. PASSWORD POLICY AND RESET
=====================================================
Password rules:
- Minimum length is 14 characters.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
- You cannot reuse your last 12 passwords.
- Passwords must include upper case, lower case, a number, and a symbol.

Account lockout:
- An account is locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts.
- A locked account unlocks automatically after 30 minutes, or immediately if the IT Service Desk unlocks it.

How to reset your password:
1. Go to https://reset.acmecorp.example
2. Enter your company email address.
3. Verify your identity using Acme Authenticator (MFA code).
4. Choose a new password that follows the rules above.
If you cannot access Acme Authenticator, call the IT Service Desk on extension 4357 (HELP). The agent will verify your employee ID and manager name before resetting.

=====================================================
3. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)
=====================================================
- MFA is required for email, VPN, and all cloud applications.
- Use the Acme Authenticator app. SMS codes are not allowed.
- If you get a new phone, raise a ticket titled "MFA device change" before wiping the old phone.
- Never approve an MFA request you did not start. Report it to security@acmecorp.example immediately.

=====================================================
4. NEW LAPTOP SETUP
=====================================================
New employees receive a laptop on day one. Setup steps:
1. Power on and connect to the Wi-Fi network "Acme-Secure" (use your company credentials).
2. Sign in and let the device finish enrollment (about 20 minutes, do not shut down).
3. Open the Company Portal and install the standard apps: Teams, Outlook, Acme SecureConnect, and the antivirus agent.
4. Enable disk encryption if prompted. Encryption is mandatory.
5. Restart the laptop when asked.
Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 1 hour.

=====================================================
5. SOFTWARE ACCESS REQUESTS
=====================================================
- Request new software through the ServiceNow "Software Request" form.
- Your manager must approve the request.
- Approved standard software is installed within 2 business days.
- Software that is not on the approved list needs a security review, which can take up to 10 business days.
- Installing unapproved software without permission is not allowed.

=====================================================
6. EMAIL
=====================================================
- Each mailbox has a 50 GB limit.
- The maximum attachment size is 25 MB. For larger files, share an OneDrive link instead.
- Automatic email forwarding to personal accounts is blocked.

=====================================================
7. PHISHING AND SECURITY INCIDENTS
=====================================================
If you receive a suspicious email:
1. Do not click links or open attachments.
2. Click the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook.
3. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, change your password immediately and email security@acmecorp.example within 1 hour.
The Security Team will contact you with next steps.

=====================================================
8. MICROSOFT TEAMS OUTAGE - INTERNAL GUIDANCE
=====================================================
If Microsoft Teams is down or very slow:
1. Check the internal status page at https://status.acmecorp.example before raising a ticket.
2. Join the backup meeting bridge on Zoom: https://zoom.acmecorp.example/backup (code 482913).
3. Post updates in the #it-outage channel in Slack, not in Teams.
4. The IT Service Desk will send an email update every 30 minutes until Teams is restored.
